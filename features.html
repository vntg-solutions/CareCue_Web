Features

Everything you need, in one calm place

CareCue is built for people who want to stay on top of their health essentials without feeling overwhelmed. Each feature is designed to be clear, quick and kind.

💧

Water intake tracking

Set a water goal that fits your life, then log drinks in seconds. A clean progress ring shows how far you’ve come and how many glasses are left.

Choose from preset challenges (7‑day, 30‑day) to build the habit gently instead of trying to change everything at once.

🌙

Sleep monitoring

Log your bed and wake times, then see a summary for “Last Night” and your rolling average. A bold, colourful card makes it easy to check at a glance.

Use gentle sleep sounds while you fall asleep and watch your average hours grow over time.

💊

Medication reminders

Add medications, choose doses and times, and let CareCue nudge you when it’s time.

Clear tracking for taken or missed doses helps you stay consistent and confident.

📅

Appointments & calendar

Track appointments and key health events right next to your daily goals so nothing slips through the cracks.

A simple calendar view and weekly comparisons show how your routines are trending.

Smart insights

Daily Health Score & suggestions

CareCue brings your logs together into a Daily Health Score so you can quickly see how today compares to your normal. When something is off, you’ll see simple, encouraging suggestions – never guilt.

  • See a clear score out of 100 based on water, sleep, meds and mood.
  • Compare this week to last week, so you can spot gentle upward trends.
  • Get text suggestions, like easing your water goal if it’s too ambitious.
Built to stay out of your way

CareCue is designed to feel like a quiet companion. You’ll get just enough reminders to keep your goals on track, without the constant noise that leads to notification fatigue.

Thoughtful notifications
Choose which reminders you want and when, then adjust them any time from the Settings screen.
Profiles & goals
Set your own daily water and sleep targets, and add family profiles for people you care for.
Privacy first

Your data stays on your device

CareCue is intentionally offline‑first. We don’t run a server that collects your health information – your logs live with you.

Local storage

All your health data is stored locally on your device. There is no cloud account to create and no background syncing of your entries.

Optional permissions

Microphone, calendar and photos are only used when you enable features like voice notes or appointment sync – and recordings stay on your device.

Transparent policy

Our privacy policy is short, plain‑language and easy to read.
